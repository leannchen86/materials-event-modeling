# Representation A/B on Severson: grammar-preserved vs paper-shaped (rung 3)

Started 2026-07-03. Ladder placement: rung 3 (slot ablations / representation A/B) of
[../spine/event_grammar_validation_note.md](../spine/event_grammar_validation_note.md),
serving top-claim candidate 3 (labels/summaries are lossy projections). Dataset: the
grammar-adapted Severson batch 1 (46 cells, L3 in the
[coverage study](event_grammar_coverage_study.md)); events regenerated by
`scripts/adapters/adapt_severson_battery.py`.

## The two representations (same source data, same models)

- **A — grammar-preserved:** intent (charge policy parameters `c_rate_1`, `soc_switch`,
  `c_rate_2`) + the per-cycle trajectory (first *k* cycles of the summary series:
  QDischarge, QCharge, IR, Tavg/Tmax/Tmin, chargetime) + outcome incl. the 10 censored
  (truncated) cells.
- **B — paper-shaped projection:** what a results table keeps — the recipe (the same
  three policy parameters) and the final number (cycle life). No trajectory, no censored
  cells (a truncated run has no final number to put in a table).

Null hypothesis (paper-shape suffices): B predicts everything A predicts.

## Tasks, baselines, splits

1. **Cycle-life regression** (log10 cycle life, 36 EOL cells): representations
   B (policy), A-traj (trajectory only), A-full (policy + trajectory), each at
   k = 10, 50, 100 observed cycles. Models: ridge and random forest (both reported).
   Baselines: train-mean. Splits: 5-fold random-cell CV and grouped-by-policy CV
   (held-out-policy), both × 3 seeds.
2. **Within-policy replicate ranking** (the decisive sub-task): for the 22 replicated
   policy groups, predict which replicate lives longer, scoring each cell with a model
   trained leave-one-policy-out. B produces identical scores within a group by
   construction → forced 0.5. Censored cells resolve a pair when the truncated record
   already outlasts the sibling's whole life — pairs only A can even express. Metric:
   pairwise accuracy with a 2,000-resample bootstrap CI over pairs.
3. **Trajectory forecast** (event-native objective, no label): predict QDischarge at
   cycle 200 and 300 from the first 100 cycles. Baselines: per-cell linear extrapolation
   fit on cycles 50–100 (the strongest cheap baseline — the oleogel campaign's "clock"
   lesson), train-mean, B (policy-only regression), A-full. 5-fold × 3 seeds.

Provenance slot: pre-registered as degenerate for *features* in this dataset (batch is
constant, channel is unique per cell); its value here is enabling honest grouped splits,
not predictive signal. No feature-level provenance gain is claimed or expected.

## Pre-registered hypotheses (committed before the run)

- **H1 (trajectory beats recipe at the label's own game).** Random-cell CV, k=100:
  A-full improves over B on Spearman by ≥ 0.10. Expected numbers: B Spearman ~0.60–0.75
  (the sweep was designed to spread lifetime), A-full ~0.80–0.90. *Falsifier:* A-full ≤ B
  → the trajectory carries no incremental signal and the thesis arm fails on this
  dataset.
- **H2 (the projection is provably lossy — the killer sub-task).** Within-policy ranking:
  B = 0.50 by construction; A-full ≥ 0.60 with a bootstrap CI excluding 0.50. Expected
  point estimate ~0.65 ± 0.10 (wide — only ~24 pairs). *Falsifier:* CI includes 0.50 →
  within-policy lifetime variation is unpredictable from early trajectory here, and the
  lossiness demonstration fails on this dataset.
- **H3 (extrapolation discipline).** Per-cell linear extrapolation wins the forecast at
  horizon 200 (short, pre-knee); the cross-cell event model (A-full) wins at 300 (the
  fade knee is nonlinear and cross-cell structure helps). Genuinely uncertain — the
  prediction is on record either way.
- **H4 (transfer).** Under held-out-policy splits, A-full's Spearman advantage over B
  shrinks (expected: by roughly half) but stays positive. *Falsifier:* A-full ≤ B
  held-out-policy → the "trajectory signal" was policy identity in disguise — the Run 015
  confound formalized, and a provenance-style shortcut inside the A representation.
- **H5 (outcome slot / censoring).** The 10 truncated cells add resolvable ranking pairs
  that B cannot represent at all. Reported as counts (pairs resolvable with vs without
  censored events); any accuracy effect is exploratory, not a claim.

**Decision this changes:** whether the grammar A/B becomes the rung-3 template applied to
further datasets (and whether downloading Severson batches 2–3 for a held-out-batch
replication is the next spend), or whether rung 3 needs a different dataset.

Contrast arm on record (no new compute): the oleogel campaign (Runs 001–008, archived)
already ran the trajectory-vs-prior version of this comparison on a homogeneous
single-protocol dataset and the trajectory arm LOST to the time prior — the negative
case. Severson tests the same question where replicate structure and outcome variation
give the grammar something to preserve.
