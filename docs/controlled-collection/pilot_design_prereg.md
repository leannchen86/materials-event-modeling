## Why this design, in one paragraph

Every requirement below was purchased by a specific result in this repo, not asserted. The
pilot's job is to test the committed top claim (claim 3: inherited labels/summaries discard
information a practitioner wants, and event representations recover it) on data *born* into
the event grammar — and to close the one gap public data measurably cannot: the July 2026
held-out-batch run showed our within-recipe ranking advantage (0.756/0.779) collapses to
chance (0.522) when the scoring model has never seen the test collection batch, and Severson's
3 batches (one holding 85% of pairs) cannot settle whether that is fixable. A deliberately
counterbalanced design with ≥4 sessions makes transfer a *powered, testable* question.

## System and factorial

**System:** calcium carbonate polymorph crystallization (CaCl2 + Na2CO3; safe, cheap, fast,
XRD-distinguishable outcomes: calcite / vaterite / aragonite / amorphous / none / mixed).
Swappable if the partner platform favors another system — the design is system-agnostic;
only the factor table changes.

**Planned conditions: 2^4 full factorial = 16 conditions** (the pilot-size stress-test
floor: 12–24-event pilots went negative; 16×3 was the first healthy region):

| factor | low | high |
| --- | --- | --- |
| reagent concentration | 0.1 M | 0.5 M |
| temperature | ambient (~22 °C) | 40 °C |
| additive (Mg²⁺ molar ratio) | 0 | 0.2 |
| mixing/aging route | fast mix, no aging | slow addition, 30 min aging |

**Replicates: 3 per condition = 48 events** (extension rule below). Replicates are *separate
executions on different days*, not split samples.

## Provenance axes and counterbalancing (the load-bearing part)

Axes, each with enough units to hold one out and still have power (the July-8 lesson —
3 batches was structurally too few):

- **Sessions: 4** (distinct days, D1–D4), 12 events each.
- **Operators: 2** (O1, O2) if the partner has two hands available; otherwise substitute a
  second instrument-configuration axis and say so in the freeze commit.
- **Reagent lots: 2** (independently prepared stock solutions L1, L2).
- **Run order:** randomized within session from a committed seed.

**Assignment scheme (deliberate, not incidental — the coverage study's L3 lesson):** each
condition's 3 replicates land on 3 *distinct* sessions via a cyclic Latin rectangle
(condition *c* → sessions {c, c+1, c+2} mod 4), so **every within-condition replicate pair is
cross-session by construction**. Operators alternate within sessions such that each
condition sees both operators; lots split 2+1 per condition, alternating which lot doubles.
Consequence: the replicate-ranking task is *natively* a session-transfer task — the pilot
analog of the question Severson could not answer. The exact 48-row assignment table is
generated by a committed script with a committed seed and included in the freeze commit.

## Per-event record (grammar v1, born at L3)

- **Intent:** full planned condition + assignment row (session/operator/lot/run-order are
  *plan*, recorded before execution).
- **Observations (minimum 4, target 6 per event — the field-budget lesson: coverage, not
  convenience):** continuous process log (video via the partner's recording infra; pH /
  temperature readings; timestamped notes as `human_note` observations) + quenched aliquots
  at t ≈ 5, 15, 60 min and final (~24 h), each dried and scanned by XRD. Measurement order
  randomized and logged (measurement session is itself a provenance axis).
- **Outcome:** status from *pre-stated physical criteria* (written here, before collection):
  no precipitate at 24 h = `failure`; precipitate but XRD uninterpretable/mixed-beyond-
  assignment = `ambiguous`; aborted execution = `aborted` with the partial record retained.
  **Nothing is silently redone** — a redo is a new event with its own run-order slot; the
  original keeps its record (the CRO failure-deletion lesson, inverted).
- **Labels:** assigned only after the raw record is frozen (freeze = manifest hash committed),
  by ≥2 labelers using standard practice terms, disagreement retained (the NIST showcase).
- **Quality flags:** field-level physical-bounds checks per modality, defined in the freeze
  commit (the Severson sensor-glitch lesson: adapters must flag, not carry, artifacts).
- **Instrument baseline:** Tier 1 of [experiments.md](experiments.md) (the same-sample
  round-robin) runs *first* and its manifests ship with the pilot — the instrument's own
  fingerprint, characterized before any science rests on it.

Conformance targets, verified by `scripts/audit_event_grammar.py` on day one and at freeze:
**Level 3**, `selection_risk.success_bias_risk` ≠ high (failures genuinely retained),
`min_independent_provenance_units ≥ 4` on the session axis.

## Pre-registered analyses (frozen at the freeze commit)

1. **The primary task (the claim-3 reversal condition).** Self-designed and frozen
   here, grounded in the system's own applied literature rather than elicited (the design
   is ours by agreement; one step weaker than practitioner-elicited, and we say so):
   **predict the 24 h polymorph outcome — vaterite fraction and failure/no-precipitate —
   from information available at t ≤ 60 min**, recipe-only (B) vs recipe + early trace
   (A). Polymorph selection under additive/temperature control is the canonical practical
   question for CaCO3 crystallization (vaterite stabilization vs calcite conversion), not
   a task chosen because labels are blind to it — and B can genuinely compete (the recipe
   factors are designed to drive the outcome). Falsifier (pre-committed in the top-claim
   decision memo): if A does not beat B here, claim 3 is "true but useless" and the
   project pivots. If the partner or any practicing chemist later names a different task,
   it is ADDED as task 1b before unblinding — never substituted after.
2. **Representation A/B, replicated on born-L3 data:** within-condition replicate ranking
   (grammar vs paper-shape; B is structurally 0.500 as always) on the practitioner-relevant
   outcome, ridge + two non-tree families, cluster bootstrap over the 16 condition groups.
   Expected pairs: 48 across 16 clusters (vs Severson's 160 dominated by 5).
3. **Held-out-session transfer (the July-8 gap, now powered):** score every cell with models
   trained only on the other 3 sessions; every pair is cross-session by design. Primary bar:
   pooled held-out-session ranking ≥ 0.70 with cluster-CI excluding 0.50. Rough power: 48
   pairs / 16 clusters resolves ~0.75 vs 0.50 but *not* ~0.60 vs 0.50 — hence the extension
   rule: if the point estimate lands in (0.55, 0.70), extend to 96 events (16×6) before any
   verdict; pre-registered, not post-hoc.
4. **Provenance audits as standard equipment:** session/operator/lot recoverability from raw
   XRD and from trajectory features (the protocol tool with grouped folds); reported
   whatever the answer, and used to decide whether analyses 1–3 need residualization.
5. **Baselines until it hurts:** recipe-only, train-mean, per-event time-prior/interpolation
   (the oleogel clock lesson), strongest per-cell extrapolation where applicable.

## Resource envelope (to confirm with the partner)

48 events × ~2 h bench time (parallelizable within session) + ~192–288 XRD scans (short
scans acceptable; Tier 1 characterizes the speed/quality trade) + video storage. Four
sessions over ~2–3 weeks. Extension option doubles this; the 16×3 core is the commitment.

## What would make this design fail honestly

- The practitioner task is won by the recipe baseline → claim-3 reversal fires (see memo).
- Held-out-session ranking ≤ chance even here → the within-corpus story from Severson
  repeats on designed data; the transferable-physics form of claim 3 is dead and the
  structural form is all that survives. That would be a real, publishable negative.
- Failure/ambiguous count = 0 across 48 events → the factorial was too tame; widen factor
  ranges in the extension (a pilot that cannot produce failures cannot test failure value).
