# Downstream-Failure Compression Program

Status: active evidence ladder, revised 2026-07-12. The
[task-relevant compression audit](task_relevant_compression_audit.md) owns definitions and
estimands; the [partner collection pipeline](../controlled-collection/partner_collection_pipeline.md)
owns operational fields and gates.

## Objective

For one delayed outcome and one action available before it arrives:

> Find the earliest verified reporting edge that loses transferable decision value, and identify
> the least costly adequate intermediate while recording whether upstream evidence remains
> recoverable.

The project is not a generic `raw versus label` comparison. It requires the actual native artifact,
transformation graph, conventional report, outcome, action, cutoff, costs, and environment units.

## Contribution boundary

Information bottlenecks, task-specific decision risk, early performance prediction, missing-data
selection, provenance, and domain-held-out evaluation already exist. The prospective contribution
is their combination at a real experimental reporting boundary:

- separate common-support risk from omitted attempts and impossible decisions;
- begin above the adapter with measurement opportunities and capture policy;
- localize loss only along verified parent-child transformations;
- require held-environment transfer before calling residual signal useful;
- distinguish failure, ambiguity, censoring, and unavailable representation; and
- optimize a risk/support/transfer/cost frontier rather than advocate universal retention.

## Evidence ladder

| phase | endpoint | evidence ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0. known controls | deliberately adequate and lossy compressions | audit mechanics work |
| 1. CaCO3 methods pilot | frozen 24-hour laboratory outcome | single-site methods evidence |
| 2. final-spec bridge | delayed property of an independent preparation | early-QC evidence across held within-site environments |
| 3. consequential study | degradation, lifetime, strength, corrosion, yield, or another partner-native property | downstream-value evidence |
| 4. external validation | frozen outcome and action at an independent site | reproducibility/deployment evidence |
| 5. decision trial | cost, delay, scrap, false accept/reject, yield, or regret | operational impact after transfer |

Each phase receives a separate preregistration and dataset. An earlier phase may size a later one;
it cannot be relabeled confirmatory.

## Partner workflow requirements

A candidate workflow must supply:

- a real early action with meaningful cost or time consequence;
- a failure-aware delayed target defined on the correct physical subject and horizon;
- measurement opportunities, native bytes, reader recipes, hashes, and capture omissions;
- every genuine intermediate and the report or grade used in practice;
- attempt, eligibility, follow-up, assay, representation, failure, and censor denominators;
- immutable event-to-batch-to-specimen/device lineage;
- multiple independent batches/environments and a path to an external site; and
- acquisition, retention, processing, annotation, and latency costs.

Access and lineage outrank fashionable chemistry. A smaller well-crossed study is stronger than
many descendants of a few confounded batches.

## Study roles

1. **Golden bundle:** nonconfirmatory mechanics check; proves files, joins, units, clocks, readers,
   reports, outcomes, and ledgers can be reconstructed. It proves no effect.
2. **Pilot:** estimates assay noise, missingness, censoring, batch variance, support, and feasible
   costs for hierarchical simulation.
3. **Confirmatory collection:** freezes the representation DAG, target, action, utility, cutoff,
   split, learner family, adequacy margins, and stop rules before outcome access.
4. **External validation and decision trial:** tests transfer first, then whether using the signal
   changes actions or outcomes.

## Decision gates

Proceed only when:

1. a known-loss and a known-adequacy control return the correct verdict;
2. the partner's real golden bundle passes and all unaudited upstream edges are priced;
3. the pilot supports cluster-aware power for both improvement and bounded adequacy;
4. the confirmatory effect survives its frozen held-batch environment; and
5. an external site reproduces it before any cross-lab or industry claim.

Stop or narrow the claim if the conventional report is adequate, only an uneconomic native artifact
works, gains are provenance-local, follow-up is irreparably selected, failures/censors cannot be
retained, or too few independent environments support the target altitude.
