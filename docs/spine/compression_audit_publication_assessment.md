# Publication Assessment — Task-Relevant Compression Audit

Status: paper-worthy protocol hypothesis; prospective validation incomplete. Revised 2026-07-12.
Formal method: [task_relevant_compression_audit.md](task_relevant_compression_audit.md). Evidence
ladder: [downstream_failure_research_program.md](downstream_failure_research_program.md).

## Verdict

This is not a new information-theory result. Task-specific decision risk, information bottlenecks,
missing-data selection, provenance, and environment-held-out evaluation have mature literatures. A
paper presenting the TRCL equation as the novelty should be rejected.

The defensible contribution is a prospective experimental-science audit that combines:

1. task risk on common support;
2. attempted-event and decision support lost by reporting;
3. collisions that force distinct alternatives to tie;
4. provenance-held-out transfer;
5. edge localization only along verified transformations;
6. capture opportunities above the native artifact; and
7. recoverability and cost of upstream evidence.

Credible positioning:

> We prospectively validate a support-aware, capture- and provenance-stressed audit of task-specific
> information loss across an experimental reporting pipeline.

Use `first` only as a qualified claim after a formal literature review. The present review did not
find one materials protocol combining all seven pieces; search absence is not priority evidence.

## Prior-art boundary

| established area | consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Blackwell comparison of experiments ([Blackwell, 1953](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177729032)) | decision-relative informativeness is not new |
| information bottleneck ([Tishby, Pereira & Bialek](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0004057)) and predictive V-information ([Xu et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10689)) | TRCL is an applied learner-bounded risk comparison, not a new information measure |
| missingness and selection ([Rubin, 1976](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/63.3.581); [Heckman, 1979](https://doi.org/10.2307/1912352)) | omitted attempts require denominators and assumptions, not a renamed accuracy metric |
| task-aware scientific compression ([Pomarico et al., 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-07445-4); [Fluty & Ludtke, 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsb.2022.107875)) | do not claim the first downstream audit of scientific compression |
| materials provenance infrastructure ([Statt et al., 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02107-0); [ESAMP](https://doi.org/10.1039/D3DD00054K)) | claim quantitative diagnosis on top of lineage systems, not invention of provenance graphs |
| materials information-loss and native-file arguments ([Iwata, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1002/csc3.70016); [Reeves-McLaren, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsomega.6c04971)) | generic compression awareness and raw-file advocacy are unavailable as novelty |
| early materials performance prediction ([Severson et al., 2019](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41560-019-0356-8); [BatLiNet](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-024-00972-x)) | predicting a delayed endpoint is not new; auditing the actual report ladder and transfer is the object |
| group/domain robustness and materials LOCO-CV ([Meredig et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.08841)) | held-environment evaluation is a requirement, not a novelty claim |

The closest empirical challenge is scientific compression work that already compares raw and
processed data through downstream distortion. The distinction must be visible in the paper:
experimental reporting can also omit entire attempts, merge decision alternatives, use undocumented
human evidence, and retain collection signal that fails to transfer.

## Evidence required

### 1. Known controls

The audit must correctly classify at least one deliberately adequate compression and one known-loss
edge. The Severson within-cycle-to-per-cycle edge is a useful retrospective positive control because
the omitted channel has a known published signal; a prospective known-mixture intervention remains
the stronger causal calibration.

### 2. Prospective methods study

The CaCO3 study can test the mechanics on a frozen laboratory endpoint, with actual attempts,
capture policy, intermediate representations, blinded reports, and held within-site environments.
It can support a single-site methods claim, not reproducibility or industrial value.

### 3. Negative and shortcut cases

The instrument must be capable of returning redundancy, support loss, complementarity,
nontransferability, bounded adequacy, and inconclusive—not merely `richer wins`. Oleogel, RRUFF, and
Severson are retrospective calibration cases, not completed prospective audits.

### 4. Consequential replication

A higher-impact paper requires a separate preregistered chain with one costly delayed outcome, one
early action, the actual report, complete physical-unit lineage, failures/censors, held material or
manufacturing batches, and a frozen external site. Improvement in yield, scrap, cost, or decisions
is claimed only after a prospective decision trial.

## Two-paper ceiling

1. **Methods/audit paper:** known controls, single-site prospective collection, support accounting,
   capture-aware edge localization, software, and held within-site environments.
2. **Downstream-value paper:** consequential outcome, risk-cost frontier, held-batch transfer,
   external-site validation, and eventually a decision trial.

Do not make the first paper carry the second paper's claim. Without prospective data, the current
work is a protocol/design paper or dataset note, not a validated industry method.

## Go/no-go

Proceed toward a methods paper only if:

- known-loss and known-adequacy controls return the correct verdict;
- every attempted or unclassifiable event remains in the denominator;
- the prospective result is interpretable even when the compact report is adequate;
- gains survive the frozen environment split; and
- released code, hashes, ledgers, and manifests reproduce every contrast.

Downgrade if the only positive is structural deletion by construction, every learned increment is
batch-local, capture above the adapter remains unknown, or adequacy is underpowered. Proceed to a
downstream-value paper only after a real partner supplies the complete opportunity-to-report-to-
outcome chain and enough independent environments to test transfer.
